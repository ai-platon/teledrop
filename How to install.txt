TeleDrop — drag files to your MacBook's notch, get them in Telegram.

INSTALL
1. Drag TeleDrop into Applications.
2. Open TeleDrop. macOS will say it can't verify the developer —
   that's normal for apps outside the App Store.
   Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
   "Open Anyway" next to TeleDrop.
3. The setup window opens: create a bot in @BotFather, paste its token,
   press Start in your bot. Done.

HOW TO USE
• Start dragging any file — an island "To Telegram" appears next to the notch.
• Move the file onto the island and let go.
• Files up to 49 MB are sent as is. Bigger ones are zipped; a zip that is still
  too big is split into .001, .002… parts (open with 7-Zip, The Unarchiver,
  Keka, or the cat command).
• The paper plane in the menu bar: settings, pick files, quit.

macOS MAY ASK FOR
• Desktop / Documents / Downloads access — to read the file you dropped.
• Screen Recording — only if Coucou is installed: TeleDrop checks whether
  Coucou is open and moves aside. You can decline; everything else works.

PRIVACY
The bot token stays on your Mac. Files go straight to Telegram, to your bot —
nowhere else.

UNINSTALL
Quit TeleDrop (paper plane → Quit) and delete it from Applications.
